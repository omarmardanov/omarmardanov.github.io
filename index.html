Case studies

Partal — a spare-parts search engine for Azerbaijan

Live at az.partal.app. Market, product, interface and code, solo — phone-and-SMS accounts, messy-spreadsheet catalogue import, free-form search across three languages in one string. Refused to be the marketplace: no commission, no checkout, the buyer just calls the seller. Seller sign-in is broken now, and the case says why I'm not fixing it.

Haven — an iOS app for the documents a household actually loses

Started as my own problem after several moves between countries, then checked whether it generalizes — it does. What people lose is not the file but the link between a document and its context: where the paper original is, when it expires, whose it is. Product, design and SwiftUI build, solo. Two features refused, and why.

Litera.Studio — site audit and rebuild

Print studio, 226 services, no CMS access handed over. Full audit from a crawl, a year of Yandex Metrika, and a raw email log — then a prototype: 218KB down to 46KB, 9.8MB down to 0.68MB. Not live yet — client hasn't seen the pitch.

Galifanov, Malkov & Partners — information architecture and a design system

Patent and law firm, 18 flat services reorganized into 4 directions plus a hidden specialization layer. One design system across 18 page templates, kept in sync by four small scripts instead of manual discipline. Handed to a WordPress developer mid-build.
